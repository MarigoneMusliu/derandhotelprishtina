Between the shows

Best places to visit in Prishtina

Coming for a festival? Make the most of your days in the city — these are the places our team sends every guest to.

Memorial

Heroinat

A woman's face formed from thousands of small metal pins, honouring the women of Kosovo who suffered and resisted during the 1998–99 war. Moving by day and striking at night, right in the city centre.

Open in Maps
Icon of independence

Newborn

Unveiled on 17 February 2008, the day Kosovo declared independence, and repainted every year.

Open in Maps
Architecture

National Library of Kosovo

Opened in 1982, famous for its dozens of white domes wrapped in a metal lattice.

Open in Maps
City views

Mother Teresa Cathedral

A bright, calm interior — and the bell tower has one of the best views over Prishtina.

Open in Maps
History

National Museum of Kosovo

An 1889 building that is home to the Neolithic "Goddess on the Throne", the symbol of Prishtina.

See it all on foot

Most of these places are a short walk apart in the city centre. Ask at the Derand Hotel reception and we'll plan the best walking route for your free day.

More to discover

Mother Teresa BoulevardThe pedestrian heart of the city, lined with cafés — Prishtina's famous macchiato culture starts here. Skanderbeg SquareThe main square with the statue of the national hero, next to the Government building. Ethnological Museum Emin GjikuA preserved Ottoman-era family house in the old town, showing traditional Albanian life. Old Bazaar & Imperial MosqueThe historic old town around the 15th-century Sultan Mehmet Fatih Mosque. Germia ParkA huge forest park on the edge of the city — hiking trails, picnic spots and a big outdoor pool in summer. Gračanica MonasteryA 14th-century UNESCO World Heritage monastery, a short drive south-east of Prishtina.
